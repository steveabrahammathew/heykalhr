Ethical Commitment

Ethical recruitment, transparency, and candidate-first professional support.

Our Commitment to Ethical Practice

At Heykal HR Consulting Services, we are fully committed to ethical recruitment and compliance with the British Columbia Employment Standards Act.

Our Fee Policy

Recruitment Services

We never charge job seekers for recruitment, job placement, or access to employment opportunities. These services are provided free of charge to candidates.


Professional Career & Settlement Support

We offer optional, paid value-added services such as personalized career coaching, resume optimization, and comprehensive newcomer settlement support.

These services are entirely a matter of choice for the candidate, are not a condition of employment, and are completely distinct from our employer-funded recruitment operations.

Resource Transparency

We believe in empowering our clients with full information. While Heykal HR Consulting Services provides specialized, intensive, and personalized support, we encourage all job seekers to explore all available community resources in British Columbia, including publicly funded programs.

We want you to be fully aware of all options available to you as you navigate your career journey.

Built on Trust & Integrity

Our commitment is simple: transparent guidance, ethical recruitment, and professional support that empowers individuals without compromising fairness, trust, or compliance.
