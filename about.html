About Us

Meet Your Consultant

Sheba Thomas

Global Expertise. Local Heart.

My journey in Human Resources began with a Bachelor’s degree specialized in HR from Madras Christian College (MCC) in Chennai, India. This foundational education sparked my lifelong passion for people management and organizational excellence.

In 2014, I relocated to Dubai, where I began my career as an HR Assistant and consistently advanced into an HR Manager role. Before leaving the UAE, I expanded my professional expertise within the manufacturing and service industry as a Customer Relationship Manager.

Seeking higher academic and professional growth, I moved to Canada to complete my Master’s in Leadership (Business Stream) at Trinity Western University.

A Vision 10 Years in the Making

A decade ago, I carried a vision of launching an independent consultancy to help others navigate their professional paths. Today, that dream is a reality with Heykal HR Consulting Services.

As an active Member of CPHR BC & Yukon, I am currently pursuing my full designation with completion expected in 2026.
