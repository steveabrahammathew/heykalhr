60-Day Success Roadmap

Why Choose the 60-Day Success Roadmap?

Relocating is stressful. We provide a structured timeline to ensure you don't miss critical steps during your transition to Canada.

Weeks 1–2

Documentation Setup:

  • SIN Application
  • MSP Registration
  • Bank Account Setup
  • Canada Child Benefit (CCB)

Weeks 3–4

Career Preparation:

  • Canadian Resume Preparation
  • LinkedIn Optimization
  • WES Educational Evaluation

Weeks 5–8

Job Search & Integration:

  • Networking Guidance
  • School Registration Support
  • Interview Preparation
